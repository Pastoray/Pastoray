<h1 align="center">Hi there 👋, I'm Reda Araar</h1>

> "What I cannot create, I do not understand." — Richard Feynman

I'm a systems and infrastructure engineer and open source contributor. I like working close to the metal, my curiosity doesn't stay in one lane, I'm drawn to whatever problem is hard, unfamiliar, or under-explained.

Most of what I know came from building things and breaking them until I understood why. That habit has taken me through compilers, debuggers, storage engines, and networking, and it's the part of the work I care about most.

---

### Open Source & Projects

* Upstream contributor to several open-source projects, including **[simdjson](https://github.com/simdjson/simdjson)** and **[Dr. Memory](https://github.com/DynamoRIO/drmemory)**.
* Author of **[PTTDB](https://github.com/Pastoray/PTTDB)**, a deterministic time-travel debugger.
* Author of **[Flume](https://github.com/Pastoray/Flume)**, an LSM-Tree storage engine in Go.
* Author of **[Peb](https://github.com/Pastoray/Peb)**, a high-performance C++ edge proxy / web server.
* Author of **[P Compiler](https://github.com/Pastoray/P)**, a dependency-free x86-64 compiler built from scratch.
